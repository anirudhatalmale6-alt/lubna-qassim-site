Profile

Lubna Qassim is an international lawyer, senior policy leader and former senior multilateral diplomat. She advises institutions, governments and boards, drawing on a career spanning law, government, financial services and international affairs.

She has led economic legislative reform, held C-suite responsibility and represented the UAE internationally. Her perspective is shaped by experience on different sides of the same decisions—where law, commercial priorities and public responsibility meet.

Beginning at Clifford Chance in London and Dubai, she built her career in cross-border legal practice before moving into economic reform. As Director of Economic Legislation at the UAE Ministry of Economy, she led a programme encompassing fourteen major economic laws to strengthen competitiveness and attract foreign direct investment. She subsequently advised governments across the GCC as a Public Sector Reform Consultant to the World Bank.

At Emirates NBD, she became the bank’s first female C-suite executive, serving as Senior Executive Vice President, Group Chief General Counsel and Group Company Secretary. She directly led a three-hundred-member team, with a remit spanning governance, legal and regulatory affairs, complex disputes and institutional transformation.

From 2018 to 2026, she served as Minister Plenipotentiary of the First Degree, having been appointed by Presidential Decree. Between 2019 and 2024, she was the UAE’s Deputy Permanent Representative to the United Nations and International Organizations in Geneva, also serving as Chargé d’Affaires during periods of that posting.

Her diplomatic work centred on coalition-building and advancing national priorities across the multilateral system. She led the UAE’s successful campaign for election to the United Nations Human Rights Council and its campaign for the Presidency of the World Meteorological Organization.

Following her return from Geneva, she served as a Senior Adviser in International Law at the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs headquarters until recently.

She is a Distinguished Fellow at UCLA’s Center for Middle East Development and serves on a number of boards across the United States, the Middle East and Europe.
