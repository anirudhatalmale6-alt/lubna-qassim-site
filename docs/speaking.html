Speaking

Conversations for a changing world

Lubna has appeared on BBC and CNN and has addressed governments, global audiences, and multilateral institutions across the United Nations system, including the World Health Organization and the Human Rights Council. She has convened roundtables at the World Economic Forum and speaks on geoeconomics, global governance, leadership, and institutional change, bringing a practitioner’s perspective to international, policy, and business audiences.

These are selected themes rather than a fixed list; I also address governance, the rule of law, and the reform of institutions.
