Mentorship

Being the mentor I never had

As I built my career, I did not have a mentor. Mentorship was neither widely discussed nor readily available, and some of the positions I held were too sensitive to make seeking counsel outside the institution straightforward.

As a corporate lawyer, C-suite executive, senior public servant and senior diplomat, I have often taken paths without a clear precedent. Too often, I was the only woman in the room, and sometimes the only woman in the office.

Those experiences were not always comfortable. Yet I am grateful for what they taught me: how to exercise judgement under pressure, find my voice in unfamiliar settings, and move forward without the reassurance of a well-trodden path.

Some of my richest learning came when I was furthest from my comfort zone.

That is why I want to offer others what I did not have: an experienced, trusted person with whom to think things through.

For Young Lawyers and Diplomats

The early years can be as much about understanding an institution as mastering a profession. I offer guidance on finding your footing, developing sound judgement, navigating unwritten expectations, and making thoughtful choices about your career.

It is a space to ask the questions you may not feel able to ask at work, and to consider not only your next role, but the professional you want to become.

For Women Advancing in Their Careers

Working hard and delivering results do not always make the next step clear. I support women seeking greater responsibility and influence: preparing for promotion, negotiating recognition and remuneration, building professional relationships, and finding their voice in rooms where they may still be the only woman.

The aim is to help you pursue your ambitions with intention, without feeling that you must become someone else to succeed.

For C-Suite Executives and Senior Leaders

At senior levels, there may be fewer people with whom you can speak openly. I offer a discreet sounding board for those carrying difficult decisions, whether managing a crisis, navigating institutional tensions, negotiating a new mandate, or considering what comes next.

Sometimes you need a different perspective. Sometimes you need someone to challenge your thinking. Sometimes you simply need to speak without having to appear certain or in control.

A Personal and Discreet Approach

I offer a limited number of one-to-one mentoring sessions, shaped around your circumstances. A space for the questions you may hesitate to ask elsewhere, the concerns you cannot easily voice at work, and the ambitions you have not yet shared.

My role is to listen carefully, challenge constructively, and help you find your own way forward, with clearer judgement and greater confidence.

One to one. In confidence.
